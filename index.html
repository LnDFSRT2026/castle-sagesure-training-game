Day 1 • Concept 2

In Interiors diagramming, what is the core difference between Segment Mode and Build Out Mode?

👆 Click to reveal answer

Answer & Rule

Segment Mode modifies the shape of an existing room. Build Out Mode builds a new room from an adjacent wall.

How can you configure the estimate view to display Actions (Remove vs. Replace) in their own separate column?

👆 Click to reveal answer

Answer & Rule

Go to the View tab in Estimate, and change the Action setting to "Separate Column". You can also choose short form.

What is the primary benefit of utilizing the Wall Editor during diagramming?

Answer & Rule

The Wall Editor lets us see a room from a 3-dimensional perspective, making it easy to place doors, windows, and cabinet blocks.
